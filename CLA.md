# Contributor License Agreement (CLA)

Omnipus accepts external contributions under a **Contributor License Agreement
(CLA)**. Before your first pull request can be merged, you (or your employer, if
you contribute on company time) sign the agreement once. After that you are
covered for all future contributions to this repository.

This CLA is adapted from the widely used Apache and Google Contributor License
Agreements. Like those, **you keep the copyright to your contribution** — you
simply grant elicify.ai Pte. Ltd. a broad license to use it.

## Why we have a CLA

Omnipus is developed by **elicify.ai Pte. Ltd.** as an open-source project under
the MIT license. Long-term, the project may also ship as a desktop variant and a
hosted Cloud / SaaS variant (see [`ROADMAP.md`](ROADMAP.md)). The CLA gives us a
clear, durable license to include your contribution in the project and in those
variants, without having to track down every contributor for permission later.

**The MIT license on the code is unchanged and irrevocable.** Anyone who
downloads Omnipus gets the same MIT terms forever. The CLA does not relicense
existing code; it only records the license you grant for your own contributions.

## What the CLA does, in plain terms

By signing, you confirm that:

1. **You keep your copyright.** You are not giving your work away — you retain
   full ownership and can use it for anything else you like.
2. **You grant a copyright license.** You let elicify.ai and everyone who
   receives the project use, modify, and redistribute your contribution.
3. **You grant a patent license.** If your contribution includes patentable
   ideas, you license those patents to users of the project.
4. **You have the right to contribute.** Either you wrote it yourself, or your
   employer has authorised it (or signed the Corporate CLA).

## How to sign

1. Open a pull request as normal.
2. The CLA bot comments on your PR with a one-click sign link.
3. Click the link, sign in with your GitHub account, and confirm.
4. Your PR is unblocked for review.

You sign once per GitHub account; all future PRs from that account are covered.

## If you would rather not sign

You do not have to. The MIT license lets you fork Omnipus, modify it, and
distribute your version freely (subject to the [trademark notice](TRADEMARKS.md)
on the name and logo). The CLA is only required to merge changes **into the
upstream Omnipus repository**.

## Common questions

- **Can I still use my own contribution elsewhere?** Yes. You keep your
  copyright and may use your contribution for any purpose.
- **What if my employer owns my work?** Have your employer sign the **Corporate
  CLA** below.
- **Will Omnipus go closed-source?** The MIT code is irrevocable — anyone who has
  it keeps the MIT grant forever. The CLA only gives us the *option* to also
  distribute the same code under other terms in future commercial variants,
  alongside the open-source MIT version.
- **Why a CLA and not just [DCO](https://developercertificate.org/)?** DCO suits
  projects that commit to a single license. Omnipus needs the option to ship
  commercial variants alongside open source, which the CLA enables.

For any other questions, email **contact@elicify.ai** or open a GitHub
discussion.
